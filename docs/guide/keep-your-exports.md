# Keep your exports

**Always keep a copy of the export files you downloaded somewhere other than Afterfeed.**

Once you close an account, the platform deletes your data and you cannot request it again. The original export is the only complete copy you will ever have.

Keeping the original files means:

- **You can re-import later.** When a new version of Afterfeed adds features, such as importing data it ignored before, you import the same files again. Afterfeed only adds what is new and keeps your edits.
- **You are not tied to Afterfeed.** If Afterfeed ever stops being useful to you, or stops working, you can move to another tool with the original data.
- **You have a second backup.** A hard drive, a second NAS or offline storage all work.

Afterfeed does not keep the archive files itself after an import. It stores what it imported (photos, videos and their metadata) in a readable folder structure on the data volume. Keeping the zip files as well would double the storage for no benefit.

## Export everything

When you request an export, select every type of data and the longest date range, even data Afterfeed does not use yet. You cannot add it later once the account is closed.

## Check the export before you close the account

After the import, the Take-in shows a report per year with the number of posts, photos and videos, and any files that could not be read. Check that every year you expect is there before you delete the account.
