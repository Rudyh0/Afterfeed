# Messages

Direct messages from your exports are kept apart from your memories. They never appear in Today or Years; you find them under **Messages** and in search results.

## What is included

| Platform | Messages |
| --- | --- |
| Facebook | Messenger conversations, including the end-to-end encrypted chats from the separate Messenger export (`messages.zip`). Both exports of the same chat are combined into one conversation. |
| Instagram | Direct messages |
| X | Direct messages |
| Snapchat | Chat history and the media that was saved in chats |

Photos, videos, voice messages and documents are shown in the conversation. Voice messages play in the conversation, with a waveform to skip through them.

PDF files show a preview of their first page. Word, Excel and PowerPoint files need the optional [document service](/configuration/document-previews); without it they show their name, type and size.

## Finding a message

- **Search in this conversation** (the magnifying glass) lists every message with the words you type. Choose one to jump to it.
- **Go to date** shows the conversation from that day on.
- The main **Search** also finds messages, across all conversations.

## Keeping a conversation private

Open the **⋯** menu in a conversation and choose **Move to vault**. See [The vault](./vault).
