# Request your Instagram export

Instagram uses the same **Download your information** flow in the Meta Accounts Center as Facebook. Menu names change from time to time; if something looks different, look for the same words.

## Request the export

1. On instagram.com, open **More › Settings**, or in the app, open your profile and the menu.
2. Open **Accounts Center** and go to **Your information and permissions › Download your information**.
3. Choose **Download or transfer information**.
4. Select your **Instagram** profile.
5. Choose **All available information**.
6. Choose **Download to device**.
7. Set the options:

   | Option | Choose |
   | --- | --- |
   | Date range | **All time** |
   | Format | **JSON** |
   | Media quality | **High** |

8. Click **Create files**.

Instagram sends you an email when the files are ready, usually within a day, sometimes longer.

## Download the files

Open **Download your information** again and download every part. The download links stay valid for a few days only.

## Then

- Import the files in the [Take-in](../take-in).
- [Keep the original files](../keep-your-exports) somewhere other than Afterfeed.
