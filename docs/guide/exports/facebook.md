# Request your Facebook export

Facebook calls this **Download your information**. It is part of the Meta Accounts Center. Menu names change from time to time; if something looks different, look for the same words.

## Request the export

1. On facebook.com, open the menu under your profile picture and go to **Settings & privacy › Settings**.
2. Open **Accounts Center** and go to **Your information and permissions › Download your information**.
3. Choose **Download or transfer information**.
4. Select your **Facebook** profile.
5. Choose **All available information**.
6. Choose **Download to device**.
7. Set the options:

   | Option | Choose |
   | --- | --- |
   | Date range | **All time** |
   | Format | **JSON** |
   | Media quality | **High** |

8. Click **Create files**.

Facebook prepares the files and sends you a notification and an email when they are ready. That takes from a few hours to several days.

## Download the files

Open **Download your information** again and download every part. Large exports are split into several zip files. The download links stay valid for a few days only.

## Encrypted Messenger chats

Since 2024, Messenger chats are end-to-end encrypted by default. Those chats are **not** in the normal export. Download them separately:

1. On messenger.com, click your profile picture and go to **Privacy & safety › End-to-end encrypted chats › Message storage**.
2. Choose **Download secure storage data** and confirm with your password.
3. Keep the page open until the download starts. You get a file named `messages.zip`.

Upload this file together with the rest of your Facebook export.

## Then

- Import the files in the [Take-in](../take-in).
- [Keep the original files](../keep-your-exports) somewhere other than Afterfeed.
