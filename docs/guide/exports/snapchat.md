# Request your Snapchat export

Snapchat calls this **My Data**. Make sure your Memories are included; they are not selected by default.

## Request the export

1. Go to **accounts.snapchat.com** and sign in.
2. Choose **My Data**.
3. Turn on **Export your Memories**, and select every other category.
4. Choose **JSON** as the format, if asked, and the full date range.
5. Confirm your email address and click **Submit**.

Snapchat sends you an email when the export is ready. That can take a day or longer.

## Download the files

Use the link in the email, or go back to **My Data › See exports**. Large exports with many Memories are split into several zip files; download all of them. The links expire after a while.

## What is included

- **Memories** come in as the original photos and videos. Captions, stickers and drawings can be separate overlay files; Afterfeed places them back on the photo. Dates and locations come from the export's JSON files, so Afterfeed adds them to each Memory.
- **Saved chats** come in as messages. Snaps and chats that were not saved are gone; Snapchat deletes them.
- **My Eyes Only** content is not included.

## Then

- Import the files in the [Take-in](../take-in).
- [Keep the original files](../keep-your-exports) somewhere other than Afterfeed.
