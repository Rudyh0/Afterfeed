# Request your X export

X calls this **Download an archive of your data**. You can only request it on the website, not in the app.

## Request the export

1. On x.com, open **More › Settings and privacy**.
2. Go to **Your account › Download an archive of your data**.
3. Confirm your password and verify your identity with the code X sends you.
4. Click **Request archive**.

X prepares the archive and notifies you when it is ready. That usually takes a day or more. Large archives with many direct messages take longer.

## Download the files

Go back to **Download an archive of your data** and click **Download archive**. Large archives can come in several parts; download all of them.

Deleted posts are not in the archive.

## Then

- Import the files in the [Take-in](../take-in).
- [Keep the original files](../keep-your-exports) somewhere other than Afterfeed.
