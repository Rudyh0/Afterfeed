# Editing metadata

Exports do not always have the right date or a location. Facebook removes the location from photos when they are uploaded, and camera clocks are sometimes wrong. The metadata editor fixes this without touching your original files.

## One memory

Open a memory, click the **⋯** menu and choose **Edit metadata**. You can change:

- **Date and time**, with the time zone it was taken in
- **Location**, by searching for a place or clicking the map
- **Description**

A changed field shows its original value underneath, with **Restore original** to undo the change. For a memory with several photos or videos, the change applies to all of them.

## A selection

In a month of the year overview or in search results, select several memories and choose **Edit metadata** in the bar at the top. For each field you choose:

- **Don't change**
- **Set** – give every selected memory the same value, for example one location for a whole trip
- **Shift** (date and time) – move every item by the same number of hours and minutes, for example when the camera was set to the wrong time zone

Memories that already have a location keep it unless you turn on the option to change those too.

## What happens to the files

The original files on your data volume are never changed. Your edits are stored next to them, together with the original values. When you download a photo or video, Afterfeed writes the edited date, time and location into the downloaded file, so it shows up correctly in other apps.

Importing the same export again does not undo your edits.
